## The One-Sentence Vision

**OffMind is a personal operating system for your thinking — capture anything, organize your way, build whatever you need. One place. Nothing lost.**

---

## The Problem

People who think constantly — entrepreneurs, creators, knowledge workers — face two broken options:

1. **Fast capture, no evolution.** Apple Notes, Bear, Google Keep. Thoughts enter but never grow. 2,000 notes, nothing to show for it.
2. **Powerful building, high friction.** Notion, Obsidian, Tana. Thoughts could grow, but you need to architect a system first. 30+ minutes of setup before a single capture.

And underneath both: **fragmentation.** Real thinking happens across 3-5 apps. Ideas captured in one place never become projects in another. The friction of switching tools kills momentum, volume, and quality. Every transfer is a leak.

Nobody solves both. The gap between "capture instantly" and "build powerfully" — without juggling tools — is where OffMind lives.

---

## The Core: Three-Layer Pipeline

```
CAPTURE  →  ROUTE  →  COMPOUND
(grab it)   (keep it)   (grow it)
```

### Layer 1 — Capture
Zero friction. Zero context-switching. Any thought, anywhere, in seconds.

- Keyboard shortcut → capture popup → done. Never leave what you're doing.
- Mobile capture in 2 taps.
- Browser extension for saving web content.
- No decisions at capture time. Optionally target a specific project/page inbox, or send to general inbox.
- The capture moment is sacred. Nothing should ever slow it down.

**What this solves:** "I had an idea but didn't write it down because opening the app was too much friction."

### Layer 2 — Route
Process when ready. Custom containers that match YOUR thinking.

- General inbox collects everything. Process on your schedule — no guilt.
- Route to custom destinations: Backlog, Questions, Ideas, Reference, Someday/Maybe, or whatever containers make sense to you.
- Project-level inboxes: capture directly to a project, process within its context.
- Items are organized, findable, and accounted for. Nothing floats in void.

**What this solves:** "I have 2,000 notes and can't find anything. Everything is one flat list."

### Layer 3 — Compound
Thoughts grow into real things. This is where complexity CAN live — but never has to.

- Items evolve: a sentence becomes subtasks, becomes a project, becomes a rich page.
- Pages with building blocks (text, headings, checklists, tables, embeds, and more over time).
- Sub-pages for complex document trees.
- Spaces and Projects for high-level organization.
- Weekly review resurfaces what's been forgotten. Nothing dies.
- The user has full creative freedom to build — but always starting from a captured thought, never from a blank page.

**What this solves:** "I keep switching to another tool to do the real work. If I could just build here, I wouldn't lose anything in the transfer."

## What Makes This "Compound"

The pipeline isn't just three steps. It's a compound system:

- **Items don't just move — they grow.** A sentence becomes subtasks, becomes a project page, becomes a knowledge document.
- **Destinations aren't folders — they're behavioral contexts.** Items adapt based on where they live.
- **Weekly review resurfaces what's dying.** Every thought gets its moment, even if it waits months.
- **Over time, the workspace reflects YOUR thinking patterns** — not a template you copied. The structure emerges from usage.

This is the core differentiator: **you never design a system. The system grows from your thinking.**

---

## The Role of AI

AI is a helper, never an actor. It serves the user's thinking — never replaces it.

**Core AI behaviors:**
- **Remind:** Surface thoughts that have been sitting too long. "You captured this 3 weeks ago. Still relevant?"
- **Connect:** Detect related items the user hasn't linked. "These 4 captures are about the same topic."
- **Suggest routes:** Propose destinations for inbox items based on learned patterns.
- **Suggest merges:** When items overlap, propose consolidation.
- **Clean:** Flag duplicates, outdated items, things that can be archived.
- **Scaffold:** When enough items accumulate on a topic, offer to create a structured page connecting them.

**AI stance:** Proposes, never acts. The user confirms every action. Trust is sacred.

**Future vision (directional, not specified):**
- Knowledge graph showing connections between thoughts, projects, topics.
- Pattern detection: "You plan best on Mondays. You capture most on commutes."
- Cross-item synthesis: "Here's everything you've thought about X, assembled from fragments."
- MCP integration: OffMind as the user's AI context vault — their personal knowledge base that any AI agent can read.

## The "Compound OS for Everyone" Framing

OffMind's founder (Paulo) built a personal system called Compound OS — a vault-based AI operating system for his entire life: work, products, thinking, finances, content. It works, but it requires technical skill (CLI, YAML, markdown, git).

OffMind is the productized version of this idea: **a personal operating system for thinking, accessible to anyone — technical or not.**

The insight: everyone deserves a single source of truth for their thinking. Not just developers who can build vault systems. Not just productivity nerds who can configure Notion databases. Everyone who thinks too much and loses too much in the process.

---

## What OffMind Is NOT

- **Not a note-taking app.** Notes are the input, not the product. The product is what notes become.
- **Not a task manager.** Tasks are one possible outcome, not the core mechanic.
- **Not a "second brain."** That category is crowded and template-dependent. OffMind's structure emerges naturally.
- **Not a simpler Notion.** It's a fundamentally different relationship: Notion assumes you're an architect. OffMind assumes you're a thinker who needs the architecture to emerge.
- **Not an AI product.** AI helps, but the system works without it. The pipeline is the product. AI amplifies it.

## The Emotional Arc

1. **First capture:** Relief. "I got it down. It's safe. I didn't have to leave what I was doing."
2. **First processing session:** Clarity. "I can see everything. It's organized. Nothing is lost."
3. **First compound moment:** Value. "That random thought from 3 weeks ago just became part of a real project."
4. **Ongoing:** Trust. "This is my single source of truth. Everything I think lives here and grows."

**The transition OffMind owns: scattered fragmentation → calm confidence → compounding value.**

---

## The Minimum Product That Someone Pays For

In priority order:

1. **Capture** — frictionless, multi-platform (web, mobile, keyboard shortcut, browser extension)
2. **Route** — custom containers, inbox processing, destinations
3. **Commit/Compound** — today view, weekly review, items evolving into pages
4. **Pages** — rich pages for deeper development (blocks, sub-pages, document trees)
5. **AI** — routing suggestions, reminders, connections, knowledge graph

Tiers 1-3 are launch. Tier 4 is fast-follow. Tier 5 is the differentiator that deepens over time.

## Principles

1. **Capture is sacred.** Nothing slows down the moment of capture.
2. **Structure emerges, never demanded.** The system grows from usage, not upfront design.
3. **AI serves, never controls.** Every AI action is a proposal. User decides.
4. **Nothing dies.** Every thought gets its moment — through reviews, surfacing, connections.
5. **Simple to start, deep when ready.** Day 1 is effortless. Month 3 is powerful.
6. **One place.** If the user has to leave to do real work, we failed.
7. **Your thinking, your structure.** No templates, no "best practices" imposed. What emerges is yours.
