```
                    BUILDING POWER
                    LOW ◄────────────────► HIGH

HIGH FRICTION │ Apple Notes, Keep   │ Notion, Tana, Obsidian
              │ Hummingbird         │ Heptabase, Anytype
              │ BrainDump, mymind   │ AFFiNE, Roam

LOW FRICTION  │ Drafts, Tot         │ *** WHITE SPACE ***

              │                     │ but still has setup cost
```

**Low capture friction + high building power = empty quadrant.** This is where OffMind lives.

---

## Executive Summary

1. **The "low friction + high power" quadrant is empty.** Every tool makes you choose: easy capture (no structure) or powerful building (high setup). Nobody does both.
2. **Market is bifurcating** — extreme simplicity (Apple Notes, Hummingbird) vs extreme power (Notion, Tana). The middle is a graveyard (Evernote, WorkFlowy, Amplenote).
3. **AI wave hasn't fixed the structural problem.** Mem raised $23.5M, rebuilt twice, still struggles. AI on top of legacy architecture doesn't work.
4. **Pricing convergence: $8-15/month.** But one-time/lifetime models generate outsized loyalty. Evernote pricing betrayal is a living cautionary tale.
5. **Capture is the moat.** The tool that owns the capture moment owns the user. Build the best capture first. Everything else expands from there.

## Tier 2 — Small/Emerging (Most Important)

### Hummingbird — Instant capture + on-device AI routing
- Very close at capture layer. Zero friction, AI categorizes into To-Do/Ideas/Reminders.
- **Stops at capture.** No building, no pages, no compound growth.
- Lesson: "No decisions" framing is powerful. Their routing categories = our Destinations in embryo.

### Unfriction — macOS overlay notes, opens in <400ms
- One hotkey, instant capture, local-only, one-time purchase.
- **Pure inbox.** Designed to feed into heavier apps, not replace them.
- Lesson: The 400ms benchmark matters. One-time pricing creates loyalty.

### Lazy — Keyboard shortcut capture from anywhere
- Close concept to our capture vision. Captures ideas, web pages, tweets with context.
- **Slow execution.** Almost no progress in a year. Users left waiting.
- Lesson: Concept resonates powerfully. Execution is the variable.

### BrainDump — Minimalist capture + AI transformation
- Capture → AI converts to summaries, to-dos, blog posts, flashcards.
- **Surface-level transformation.** Output formats, not structural growth.
- Lesson: Users want captured thoughts to become useful quickly.

### Kortex → Eden — Second brain for writers
- Chat-like capture, reusable Elements, AI workflows. Now pivoting to "Eden."
- **Pivot = soft failure signal.** Writer-specific positioning too narrow.
- Lesson: Audience-first positioning (writers only) constrains growth.

### AFFiNE — Open-source Notion + Miro hybrid
- Strong building power. Block editor + infinite canvas + databases.
- **No capture philosophy.** Full workspace app, complex to configure.
- Lesson: Open-source drives discovery/trust. Complexity without opinionated defaults = friction.

### mymind — AI visual bookmarking
- No folders, no tags, AI figures it out. Beautiful.
- **Saves ≠ thoughts.** External content only, no creation, no building.
- Lesson: "AI figures it out" UX is proven and loved. Visual display is emotionally engaging.

### Napkin AI — Text-to-visual diagrams, "Idea Swarm"
- $19.5M raised, 5M+ users. Ideas float visually with AI clustering.
- **Output is diagrams for others**, not a thinking OS.
- Lesson: "Ideas floating and finding connections" UI metaphor is powerful.

## Tier 4 — Failed Attempts

| Product | What Happened | Lesson |
|---------|---------------|--------|
| **Evernote** | First mover, declined. Pricing hikes, slow evolution, trust lost. | Being first isn't a moat. Pricing trust is sacred. |
| **Roam Research** | Cult following → community collapsed. Too technical, no mobile. | Power users alone don't sustain. Need mainstream onboarding. |
| **Mem.ai** | $23.5M raised, rebuilt twice, still struggling. | AI alone doesn't solve the problem. Capture-first, AI-second. |
| **Logseq** | Open-source Roam alt. Development stagnant since funding. | Dev-first tools lack polish for mainstream. |
| **Clover Notes** | Notes+whiteboard+tasks. Acqui-hired by Figma, shut down. | Concept validated (Figma bought the team). Execution incomplete. |
| **Rewind AI → Limitless** | Passive capture of everything. Acquired by Meta. | Passive capture has consent/privacy problems. Active > passive. |
| **WorkFlowy** | Infinite outliner. Never evolved. | Single-paradigm tools hit a ceiling. |

## Strategic Insights

1. **The "pipeline" framing is the differentiator.** Nobody positions around HOW thinking moves. Everyone positions around what you can do inside the tool. Capture → Route → Compound is a new mental model.

2. **Capture is the moat, not the editor.** Drafts has a cult following despite zero building power — it won the input moment. Win capture first.

3. **"Structure emerges from usage" is genuinely novel.** Every high-power tool requires upfront decisions. This directly attacks the #1 churn reason.

4. **AI should route, not organize.** Mem/Reflect/mymind tried "AI organizes for you" — felt opaque. Better: AI suggests routing, you approve, structure builds from your decisions.

5. **Clover acqui-hire by Figma validates the space.** Figma paid for the team building exactly this concept.

6. **One-time launch pricing is the trust play.** Evernote trauma + subscription fatigue = founding-tier one-time payment creates the right signal.

7. **The real competitor is inertia** — the scattered stack of Apple Notes + Notion + camera + browser bookmarks. Position against the situation, not a product.
