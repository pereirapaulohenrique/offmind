# OffMind — Target User Research (March 2026)

**Date:** 2026-03-11
**Source:** Web research — Reddit, Medium, app reviews, surveys, market data
**Vision reference:** VISION-v2.md

---

## The Ideal First User

A **28-35 year old knowledge worker in a creative or analytical role** (designer, PM, marketer, consultant, developer, writer). Mid-size company or independent. Mac + iPhone. Technically literate but not a developer. ADHD traits or strong creative-associative thinking — brain generates ideas faster than any system can capture.

Has tried Notion (overwhelmed by setup). May still use Obsidian or Bear with mild dissatisfaction. Follows at least one productivity creator. Believes the right tool exists, just hasn't found it.

**Not looking for another tool. Looking for the last one.**

Will pay $10/month without negotiating. Will tell three people. Will write: "finally found what I needed."

## Five User Segments

### 1. The Exhausted Collector (PRIMARY — largest segment)
- **Who:** 26-38, knowledge worker, 8-20 apps installed, none working as unified system
- **Pain:** "I end up with dozens of note takers but none being a dedicated space"
- **Tried:** Notion, Obsidian, Bear, Evernote, Google Keep, Apple Notes, Craft
- **Wants:** One place that works without configuration. Capture fast, organize itself.
- **Design:** Clean, minimal, opinionated. Not blank canvas. Tool should have a point of view.
- **Pays:** $7-15/month or lifetime $49-99
- **Size:** Very large — the 70%+ of Notion's 100M users who signed up and drifted away

### 2. The System Builder (SECONDARY — influential)
- **Who:** 22-35, developer/researcher/productivity creator, Obsidian power user with 40+ plugins
- **Pain:** "I spent more time organizing than writing"
- **Tried:** Obsidian deeply, Roam, Logseq, Tana, Heptabase
- **Wants:** Powerful defaults, infinite depth if you want it. No maintenance tax.
- **Design:** Premium, dark, technical. Respects intelligence. Not playful.
- **Pays:** $10-20/month or lifetime $149-200+
- **Size:** Medium — but extremely vocal. They write reviews, post YouTube videos, recommend tools. Acquiring them is a distribution strategy.

### 3. The Cognitively Overwhelmed Creative (HIGH VALUE — emotional buyers)
- **Who:** 25-40, freelancer/designer/writer/entrepreneur, ADHD-adjacent
- **Pain:** "My ADHD brain is a sensory tornado" / "Frees up my brain"
- **Tried:** mymind, Notion (gave up), Bear, Apple Notes, physical notebooks, voice memos
- **Wants:** Zero-friction capture above all. Retrieval without remembering where you put it. Calm interface.
- **Design:** Warm, beautiful, calm. Aesthetic of a well-organized studio. Colors matter. "Beautiful" is their #1 design word.
- **Pays:** $8-12/month. High loyalty once it "clicks" — switching is emotionally costly.
- **Size:** Medium-large. ADHD apps market valued at hundreds of millions and growing.

### 4. The Quiet Life Organizer (LARGE — hardest to reach)
- **Who:** 30-50, non-technical, teacher/manager/nurse/parent-entrepreneur
- **Pain:** "I don't want to set up a system. I just want to not lose things."
- **Tried:** Apple Notes (primary), Google Keep, Notion (gave up)
- **Wants:** Dead simple. Available everywhere. Searchable. Works like a paper notebook with search.
- **Design:** Familiar, unintimidating, warm. NOT dark mode. Makes them feel competent immediately.
- **Pays:** $5-10/month or one-time under $50
- **Size:** Very large (most people) but not in PKM communities. Passive discovery only.

### 5. The Researcher / Academic (NICHE — high value)
- **Who:** 24-40, PhD/consultant/UX researcher/analyst
- **Pain:** "Spent nearly an hour searching for a saved quote, only to abandon the effort"
- **Tried:** Obsidian, Zotero, Roam, Heptabase, citation managers
- **Wants:** Bidirectional links, graph view, cross-context connections. Not just capture — synthesis.
- **Design:** Functional over beautiful. Respects density. Dark mode acceptable.
- **Pays:** $10-20/month. Most willing to pay if tool improves research output.
- **Size:** Niche but high-value and vocal.

## Design Preferences (Evidence-Based)

**WINNING: Warm Minimal**
Tools gaining most enthusiasm (Reflect, Capacities, mymind, Bear, Craft) share:
- Clean typography like a well-designed book
- Subtle warmth — off-whites, warm grays, earthy accents
- Breathing room, no clutter
- Opinionated structure (not blank canvas)
- "Beautiful without trying too hard"

User words when loving design: "beautiful," "clean," "simple," "doesn't get in the way," "feels like my mind," "joy to use," "soft and relaxing," "warm and inviting"

**ALSO WORKS: Premium Dark**
For developers/power users (Arc, Raycast, Linear aesthetic):
- Dark, premium, fast, dense
- "Snappy," "respects my workflow," "powerful without being loud"
- Performance = design quality proxy. Slow + beautiful loses to fast + decent.

**NOT WORKING:**
- Blank-canvas flexibility (Notion) — actively complained about
- Gamification (badges, streaks) — associated with consumer/habit apps, not serious tools
- Cluttered interfaces — trigger abandonment

**For OffMind:** Go warm minimal with premium texture. Craft/mymind energy but more structured. iOS-quality typography. Generous whitespace. Color that feels personal, not corporate.

## Naming Insights

**Winning pattern:** Single evocative real word. Not descriptive, not compound, not coined.
- Obsidian (permanence), Bear (warmth+strength), Notion (mental impression), Roam (movement), Craft (care), Reflect (introspection)

**Why it works:** Users are identity-expressive about their tools. Abstract names let users project meaning. They age well. "AI Knowledge Hub" is already dated. "Notion" will be fine in 20 years.

**Would "OffMind" work?**
- YES: Captures the job-to-be-done ("get it off your mind"). Rhymes with user language ("frees up my brain"). Memorable, short, distinctive.
- RISK: "Off" can signal "turned off" / "not working." May feel abstract to Quiet Life Organizers. May feel casual to System Builders vs. Obsidian/Tana.
- VERDICT: Works best if positioned around cognitive relief. Lands strongest with Exhausted Collectors and Overwhelmed Creatives. Needs clear copy for less PKM-fluent users.

**Alternative naming energy (not recommendations, just direction):** Single word, slightly physical metaphor, evokes mental clarity. Examples of the energy: Anchor, Marrow, Canopy, Meridian, Hearth, Grove, Ledge.

## What Makes Them Pay

| Trigger | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Lost something important** | #1 trigger. "I know I saved that" fails. Immediate willingness to pay. |
| **Tool that "clicks"** | Rare. "Finally found what I needed." Price becomes secondary. |
| **Life transition** | New job, going freelance, new project. Natural "I need a system" moment. |
| **Subscription fatigue** | Tired of $10/mo × 12 apps. One-time deal creates urgency + loyalty. |

**Price sweet spot:** $8-12/month subscription or $49-99 founding one-time.
**Free tier:** Non-negotiable for trust and trial.

---

## What Makes Them Leave

1. **Setup never ends** — tool feels like a job, not relief (#1 reason)
2. **Can't find things later** — retrieval failure erodes trust
3. **Slow performance / offline failure** — latency is a dealbreaker for capture tools
4. **Complexity exceeds return** — "Notion stopped being a tool and became a tax"
5. **Better-designed tool appears** — PKM users are NOT loyal, they're searching
6. **Data lock-in anxiety** — "Can I export if this company dies?"
7. **Feature creep in wrong direction** — "They're building for enterprise, not for me"

**Critical window:** If no daily habit by week 6, they won't form one. "This works" moment must happen in first 10 minutes.
