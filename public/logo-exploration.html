Recommendation

Primary: Direction 2 (OM Monogram) — Works at every size without needing two versions. The initials create instant memory. Most defensible as trademark. The geometric execution pushes it toward SaaS/tech, away from spiritual/wellness.

Fallback: Direction 1 (Interrupted Infinity) — Lowest risk evolution from current mark. The gap + animation story is genuinely distinctive. Keeps existing brand equity.

Wild card: Direction 4 (Capture-Release) — The bracket + dot is the most product-aligned metaphor and the most minimal. Could be extremely strong if Paulo wants something that breaks from the infinity concept entirely.
