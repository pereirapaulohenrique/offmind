OffMind / Codex Critique

2B has the right instinct, but the wrong silhouette.

The compact loop is the closest Claude got because it finally stops relying on pure infinity logic. But it still asks too much of the viewer at small sizes. The next move is sharper geometry, a stronger gap, and one concept that survives at 16px.

Position: do not ship 2B as-is. It reads like a generic SaaS monogram badge, not a calm thinking brand with a precise promise. If you stay in the OM family, make the gap unmistakable and the M legible. If you leave the monogram family, the best path is a mark that shows a thought being removed, not a loop being stylized.

Why 2B Stalls

Reading Failure

At hero size it can be explained as OM. At 32px it reads as a badge. At 16px it stops reading as letters and collapses into a tiny anonymous blob.

Gap Too Weak

The brand story is the release point. In 2B the gap feels accidental, not authored. The mark still feels closed, contained, and inward.

Tech Monogram Trap

Rounded container plus angular inner peaks is one of the most generic mark grammars in SaaS, crypto, and dev tools right now. It does not feel ownable enough.

Wrong Emotional Weight

OffMind should feel lighter after the system does its job. 2B feels compact, sealed, and technical. It stylizes the tension instead of resolving it.

New Directions

Recommendation

Primary: Detached Thought. It is the most ownable move away from generic infinity logic, the cleanest story at small sizes, and the easiest to animate without feeling busy. If you want initials: take Notched OM, not 2B. It keeps the compact monogram instinct but gives it a readable silhouette and a gap that actually matters. Skip: any direction that still depends on a loop reading as both infinity and OM.
