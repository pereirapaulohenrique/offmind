The Problem: Teal + White = Clinical

Current: pure white #fafafa — reads medical/clinical
Sand #e8dcc8 — immediately warmer

Why it looks medical

  • Teal + pure white + dark bg = hospital signage palette. It's the same color scheme as health apps (Calm, Headspace, medical dashboards).
  • Fixing the text alone may be enough — sand or warm grey breaks the clinical association immediately.
  • Alternatively, warming the background (#0f0e0c instead of #0a0a0a) also helps. The two combined (warm text + warm bg) move it fully into "personal workspace" territory.

Top Picks

  • Sand (#e8dcc8) — Most warmth. Teal + sand = earth + water. Feels personal, organic, Caregiver. My top recommendation.
  • Cream (#f0ebe3) — Safest. Barely tinted but enough to kill the clinical edge. If sand feels "too warm", this is the fallback.
  • Teal ff + Sand mind — If you liked the v3 color-split idea but want it on v2 Precise base. Encodes meaning.
  • Zinc/Stone — Modern but still slightly cold. Better than white, but doesn't solve the warmth problem as decisively.
  • Teal Mono — Beautiful for accents but may lack contrast for primary logo use.
