A ~300° arc with a deliberate gap. The gap IS "Off" (release). The loop IS "Mind" (thoughts). Name encoded in form. Maximum simplicity — one stroke, one gesture.

Concept 6 The Unboxed ANTI-NOTION

The direct opposite of Notion's N-in-a-cube. Four corner fragments of a rounded rectangle, pulling apart — the box is dissolving. Structure that lets go instead of containing. Where Notion says "here's a box, fill it," OffMind says "the box was never needed."

Concept 6B The Unboxed "off" NEW

The dissolving box encloses "off" — the word is still inside the breaking structure, about to be released. "mind" sits outside, already free. The logo IS the product promise: taking things off your mind means breaking out of the rigid box.
