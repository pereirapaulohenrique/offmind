Honest Assessment

  • Grey loop looks sophisticated in the wordmark — at display size, on dark bg, the warm grey + sand combo has a quiet luxury feel. It works.
  • Grey loop FAILS the favicon test. At 16px on a dark background, the grey loop nearly disappears. The teal loop stays visible. This alone is a dealbreaker for a brand mark.
  • The "medical" problem is in the text, not the loop. Swapping white → sand text already kills the clinical feel, even with a teal loop. You don't need to sacrifice the mark's visibility to fix the palette.
  • If you still want grey energy: The "grey loop + teal ff + sand mind" is the most interesting hybrid. But it has 3 colors in one wordmark — complex to maintain across contexts.
  • Bottom line: Keep teal loop (it's your 16px identity). Fix the medical feel with sand/cream text. The problem was never the teal — it was teal + pure white together.
